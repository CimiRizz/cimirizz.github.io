Organizational Maturity – From Strategy to Sustainable Value

How organizations build the capabilities to deliver value consistently.

From strategy to everyday decisions

This book examines how governance, leadership, skills and working practices influence what an organization can deliver. It connects strategic priorities to portfolio decisions, service delivery and the way teams work.

Assessing and improving capability

The book looks at how organizations use measurement and feedback to identify improvement priorities. Project management is one example alongside service management, people development and continuous improvement.
