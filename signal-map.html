Signal Map

See your serving cell and nearby mapped cells on your phone.

Download Android APK

Serving cell

The serving-cell view shows the cell currently reported by the device. When a mapped position is available, the app can show that location and its distance from you.

Reading the map

The map uses separate colors for the network operator and radio technology. Nearby mapped cells provide context for the current connection. Cell locations depend on available reference data.
