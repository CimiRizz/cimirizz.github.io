Kimmo Rissanen

I lead cross-functional teams through enterprise rollouts, software releases and service transitions. My background combines IT delivery, program governance and hands-on software quality.

Selected IT delivery work

Six examples from enterprise IT, industrial data, geospatial systems and mobile software. Each shows the delivery challenge, my role and what the work enabled.

Enterprise transformation

SAP S/4HANA rollout

The rollout brought together three streams of work that had to line up before deployment. Interfaces needed to be ready, migrated data had to be available, and business teams needed both to test their processes.

I coordinated work across integration, data migration, testing and deployment. I worked with technical specialists and business teams to prepare the interfaces and data for testing, so rollout preparations could take those dependencies into account.

Program governance

Portfolio and delivery governance

In a program valued at approximately €15 million, portfolio planning meant looking across projects at the same time. Delivery commitments depended on team capacity, and a risk in one project could affect plans elsewhere.

I worked on portfolio planning, capacity and risk management and developed a Delivery Framework and Handbook. These gave teams a common reference for defining projects, planning the work and reviewing delivery.

Industrial data

Making production-line data usable

Production-line sensors were already a source of data. The practical challenge was getting that data into a form teams could use to monitor equipment performance.

I worked on a solution that brought sensor data into view for monitoring. That gave teams a basis for using production data in overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) reviews and continuous improvement work.

Verification and validation

Scaling test automation at Nokia

Testing Nokia S60 software involved repeatable checks that had to run across releases. The work called for both a coordinated test team and automation that could take on more of those checks.

I led test teams of up to 12 people and helped increase automation coverage to approximately 80% in the S60 testing program. Routine checks could then run automatically as part of verification and validation.

Geospatial software delivery

Multi-vendor GIS delivery at Esri

At Esri Finland, I led international IT and GIS projects that brought several vendors together. The work covered hosted services and mobile applications, with delivery spanning software, hardware and customer support.

I managed delivery across the vendors and worked on quality planning. I used Agile practices to coordinate development and brought verification and validation into the delivery work, alongside the teams building the solution.

Digital workplace

SharePoint Online migration

Moving a digital workplace to SharePoint Online involves coordinating the technical migration with the teams that rely on SharePoint for everyday work. Access and support need to be considered alongside the move itself.

I worked on SharePoint Online migration as part of IT service delivery. The work brought together migration, deployment and the handover to service support.

Leading technical teams

My experience includes leading distributed delivery and test teams and coordinating work with business stakeholders, engineering specialists and service teams.

  • Plan the work. Agree on scope, milestones and responsibilities. Bring integration, test and deployment dependencies into the plan early.
  • Make decisions visible. Review delivery risks and capacity with the people who can resolve them.
  • Prepare for handover. Bring operations into release planning so support teams know what is changing and who owns it.
