Uutisvirta

Browse headlines from Ilta-Sanomat and Iltalehti in one feed.

Choose your sources

Select IS, IL or both. The feed shows the source with each story so readers can tell which publication it came from.

Read the original

Open an article on the publisher’s own site. The mobile-friendly view is designed for quickly scanning headlines and moving to a story that interests you.
