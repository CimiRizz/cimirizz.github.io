Book project

Beyond Project Management – Where Project Success Begins

A practical book about project leadership before kickoff.

Sales, definition and planning

The book starts where project commitments are made. It looks at how sales and delivery teams can clarify what the customer needs, test assumptions and translate promises into a workable scope.

What to agree before kickoff

It covers early decisions about schedule, resources, budget, dependencies and risk. The aim is to give the people who will deliver the project a chance to shape the plan while there is still room to change it.
