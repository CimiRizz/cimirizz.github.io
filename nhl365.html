NHL365

Follow an NHL team’s schedule and news from one page.

Open NHL365 (opens in a new tab)

Team schedules

Choose a team to see its next games alongside the latest team news. The schedule gives you a quick view of what’s coming up without switching between separate pages.

Local and venue time

Switch between your local time and the venue’s time zone. This is especially useful when following North American games from Europe.
